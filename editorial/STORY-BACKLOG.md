# Delaware Beach Finds — Editorial Backlog

`data/stories.json` with `status: draft` when you start work on it.

reader cares · likely sources · visual opportunity · timing.

## Known asset gaps blocking specific pitches

- **Archival/historical imagery.** The history pitches above have no in-repo archival images. Public-domain sources (Library of Congress, NARA, Delaware Public Archives) need a rights check and a download workflow before a history piece can carry a genuine period visual. Until then history features use the series' illustrated treatment.
- **Homes & Design photography.** No interiors or architectural frames exist in the library. Pitches 27–28 cannot be illustrated today.
- **Lewes photography.** The tide station, the osprey cam and the marsh cover story are all Lewes, and there is no verified Lewes frame in the library.
