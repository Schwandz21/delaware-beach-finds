How we work

Editorial independence.

Businesses can sponsor Delaware Beach Finds inventory, and we are glad when they do. Anything paid for is clearly identified as sponsored wherever a reader meets it.

What stays with us is editorial judgment: which stories we report, what we conclude, and what we recommend. That independence is the reason the audience is worth reaching, and we protect it in a partner's interest as much as our own.

We do not sell coverage, conclusions or placement inside unlabelled editorial. We do not publish claims we cannot support, about a partner or about ourselves.

Our full editorial and affiliate disclosure sets this out in plain terms.
